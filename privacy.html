Privacy Policy

Arc Repel · Last updated: October 8, 2026

Arc Repel is an offline, single-player game provided by Jiang Fuqin. This policy covers the game, this support website, and emails you send for help.

Game data stays on your device

The game does not require an account or send gameplay data to a developer server. It saves your best score, stars, greenhouse growth, creature discoveries, workshop upgrades, and audio, haptic, and visual preferences locally on your device.

Deleting the app removes its local game data. A device backup managed by your operating system may contain app data; backup and restoration are controlled by your device settings.

Advertising, tracking, and permissions

The game contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs and offers no in-app purchases. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photo library.

Support emails

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your request and troubleshoot reported problems. Please avoid sending passwords, payment information, or unrelated sensitive information.

You can contact us to request deletion of support correspondence we hold. Our email service provider processes messages to deliver this support service.

Visiting this website

This website has no analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels, or forms. It is hosted on Vercel. The hosting provider may process connection information, such as your IP address, browser information, and requested page, to deliver and secure the website. Website hosting is separate from the offline game.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from players, including children. Support emails should be sent by an adult when appropriate.

Updates and contact

We will update this page if the game's data practices change. For privacy questions, contact 704362412@qq.com.
