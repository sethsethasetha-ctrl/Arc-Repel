Arc Repel

Shield the last starflower.

Aim a curved shield around the last living core. Block incoming creatures, let green seeds through, and grow your garden among the stars.

Your voyage

  • Choose your run: defend the core in a 60-Second Voyage or keep growing in Endless Garden.
  • Read the rebound: block a Boss, then turn your shield toward its faster return path.
  • Build your defense: choose upgrades between sectors, spend stars at supply stops, and buy permanent starting boosts in the Workshop.
  • Grow and discover: nurture the greenhouse as you play and meet Bosses to reveal their colors in the Codex.
